Why Sevens Lacrosse?

One-stop shop for complete athlete training.

Structured and documented training that accelerates player development. Sevens Lacrosse prepares players to become complete athletes who are ready to meet the demands of the sport and play competitively long term.

Players who join Sevens Lacrosse are evaluated and placed into our proprietary 7s Player Progression System [7s PPS]. This structured approach makes it easy for coaches, parents, and players to understand how the athlete is progressing, what the next priority is, and how the training plan is being adjusted.

Our reporting system tracks the player's skill level and outlines the training plan. The plan is comprehensive, personalized, and constantly adjusted based on the player's commitment, progress, and training consistency.

Coaching Services

Personalized Lacrosse Training

7s Lacrosse offers private, small group, lacrosse performance, and online training for youth and high school players who want to improve their skills, athleticism, confidence, and understanding of the game.

Every session is built with a purpose. Athletes are not just running through random drills. They are working on specific skills, learning why those skills matter, and building habits that carry into games.

One-on-One

7s Private Lacrosse Training

One-on-one training for athletes who want focused coaching and individual attention.

Private sessions are built around the athlete's age, position, experience level, confidence, and goals. Training may include catching, throwing, cradling, shooting, dodging, footwork, stick protection, ground balls, off-ball movement, and position-specific skill work.

Private training is best for players who need detailed instruction, extra reps, confidence, or a clear development plan.

Private training can focus on:

Stick skills
Shooting and finishing
Dodging and footwork
Passing and catching
Ground balls
Off-ball movement
Position-specific skills
Lacrosse IQ
Confidence & game prep

7s Small Group Training

Small group training is built for siblings, friends, teammates, or parent-organized groups.

Players Need More Than Stick Skills

7s Lacrosse Performance Training

This service category is for developing speed, mobility, strength, conditioning, footwork, as well as basic nutrition to support training.

Sessions can include movement prep, change of direction, balance, acceleration, deceleration, shooting mechanics, dodging footwork, lacrosse-specific athletic development, and simple fueling education.

Basic nutrition support is included only as general athlete education — not medical advice or a strict diet plan. The goal is to help athletes understand simple habits such as hydration, pre-training fueling basics, post-training recovery, and consistent eating around practices and games.

Lacrosse Performance Training is best for players who want to become stronger, faster, and more confident on the field.

About Sevens Lacrosse

We offer a personalized "one-stop shop" for lacrosse, speed, and strength training. Whether in person or online, we provide a complete training ecosystem that helps athletes stay consistent year-round with a dedicated coach who cares about their growth.
The name "Sevens" holds special significance for our founder, Coach Alex:
  • Birth Date: Coach Alex was born on July 7th (7/7).
  • Jersey Number: He has worn #77 since high school, and it has become a central part of his identity as an athlete.
  • 7v7 Format: He believes the 7v7 format is excellent for youth development, offering more touches on the ball and helping players grasp the basics of the game, whether playing field, indoor box, or 6v6 formats.
  • Lucky 7: It is considered a lucky, divine number.
We are an independently owned business. All our coaches are USA Lacrosse Bronze Certified and committed to ongoing professional development through their courses.
